A quiet afternoon, an urge to learn Chinese resurfaced. I feel like English isn't enough anymore, it doesn't grant me any advantages in my career. I love German, but I feel the job pool related to German in VN is small. And if you mean moving to work in Germany, to be honest, I am not ready to leave my family with such huge distance. I asked for advice from ChatGPT and my friend, the conclusion is Chinese suits me. And ChatGPT enlightened me about the approach, that I crave the depth of Chinese culture, not the industrial, shallow "nihao". You know what I mean, the green owl!

I planned to write this post in Vietnamese first, but ChatGPT suggested me to write a post a week to enhance my English writing skill, so here we are.

Because my energy level is low, and I spent most of them at work, so the friction is too much I can't bear. I want it to be short but still meaningful. To do that, I must have a clear methodology and schedule.

## Research on Materials

Ok, let's move on the materials I will use to study Chinese.

### Apps

From the recommendations of ChatGPT and blogs on Internet or apps on Play Store. I tried various of app and here is my summarization.

| App            | Choose?  | Reason                                                                                                                                         |
|----------------|----------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Duolingo       | ❎      | The app has become villain, shallow and the premium version doesn't worth it                                                                      |
| Busuu          | ❎      | I bought a year subscription to learn German, but it doesn't built to teach Chinese, lack a lot of important parts                             |
| Memrise        | ✔️      | I have lifetime version so I just continue using it, it's useful to extend vocabulary                                                          |
| Anki           | ❎      | I need to collect the decks... it's just costing too much effort to start learning                                                             |
| HelloChinese   | ❎      | I tried it for a few days, it's solid but I choose SuperChinese instead                                                                        |
| SuperChinese   | ✔️      | I bought lifetime version of it, it teaches pinyin, strokes, grammar,... I think it has enough abilities to become the main structure to study |
| Todaii Chinese | ✔️      | main reason is I bought its lifetime version, lol. I think it provides various Chinese reading content without my effort to find               |

### Others

Serious learning always demands textbooks, I believe so. So I bought a grammar textbook, a writing guidebook and a blank notebook to practice.

Why I buy these books? First, they are affordable, they are thin and accomplish-able! Notice that I am self-studying, so reducing friction is important. But currently I rarely open my books, this is a point I need to plan and improve in the future. Will tell you the result in upcoming posts.

## In-Action

The principle I will apply is: **habit**. I won't dedicate a solid time block for learning but I will do it daily and try to reach minutes goal.

The core of the learning is **SuperChinese** app. I will try to learn on this app for about **20 minutes/day**.

Then **Memrise**, the expectation will be a revision and a new lesson. **TodaiiChinese** will be used to do mock exam and learn hsk1 new word daily from an article.

At every weekend, I will do mock exam on **TodaiiChinese** to have a good reflection on my current level.
