In this post, I want to talk about self-study through my lifelong journey. The inspiration of this topic came from a moment I was self-studying Chinese on SuperChinese app (in case you missed it, [here is the post I talked about start learning Chinese](https://vtrnnhlinh.github.io/blog/2026/self-study-chinese-part-0/)). I feel struggled on this journey; it isn't easy like when I self-studied English. Then I realize it took me years on the journey to improve my English, a lot of methods and learning apps/websites are used to reach current level, and I still have a long journey to master it.

## I don't know what I don't know

I have no interests in yapping about Dunning-Kruger effect, everyone knows this effect nowadays. I want to point out that it isn't easy to learn something on your own. Sometimes you will read the articles say that self-studying in this AI and digital age is easy, but it's harder than you thought. Yes, it's more approachable in this age, but it is not that kind of easy. It's like finding a specific patch of water in a vast ocean.

### Where to start?

What is the first thing you do when you want to self-study something? I am curious about your approach. Tell me if you wanna share in Comments :wink:.

Finding a starting point on this journey is an art. If I want to learn English, I will search "How to learn English?". If I want to learn Chinese, I have to type "自学中文". And if I want to dig in deeper an academic topic in Computer Science field, I will visit ArXiV and find "X survey in Year Y".

But how can you search "How to learn English?" when you don't know English yet, how can you know ArXiV and how to find reliable sources in first place?

### How to start?

Even when you finally found a seems solid guide on internet, the next question is 'does it also work for me?'.

Of course, as a person used to self-study, I know which methods are suitable for me, but to a new starter, how can?

I think there is no specific answer for this question, that you have to take time to try and figure it out. *Good things take time*.

For example, ChatGPT recommended me some courses to buy and learn Chinese. But I didn't buy any courses, not because I dont trust the quality of the courses AI recommended, but I know watching videos is hell for me. So I switch to try a lot of apps to know what suits me.
