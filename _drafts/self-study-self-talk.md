In this post, I want to talk about self-study through my lifelong journey. The inspiration of this topic came from a moment I was self-studying Chinese on SuperChinese app (in case you missed it, [here is the post I talked about start learning Chinese](https://vtrnnhlinh.github.io/blog/2026/self-study-chinese-part-0/)). I feel strugged on this journey, it isn't easy like when I self-studied English. Then I realize it took me years on the journey to improve my English (and I still have a long journey to master it).

## You don't know what you don't know

I have no interests in yapping about Dunning-Kruger effect, I want to point out that it isn't easy to learn something on your own. Sometimes you will read the articles say that self-studying in this AI and digital age is easy, but it's harder than you thought. Yes, it's more approachable in this age, but it is not that kind of easy.
